Dear sweetieee,

with all my heart

Sometimes I wish I could gather up all the little ways I love you and place them right in your hands. The warmth, the admiration, the quiet gratitude. Since I can’t, I made you this.

Saad, loving you feels like finding a softness I want to protect. I love the person you are, and the person you’re still becoming. I love that I get to care about your days, cheer for your hopes, and hold you in my thoughts even when we’re doing our own ordinary things.

I don’t need a grand occasion to say it, but I’m glad to have this one: I am grateful for you. For your heart. For every moment that has brought us here. You are precious to me in a way I’m still finding words for.

May what we share always be met with patience, honesty, gentleness, and the kind of love that brings us closer to what is good. I’ll keep choosing you with care — in the big promises and in the tiny everyday ones.

All my love, always.
